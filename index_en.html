Interactive Map of the Soil Biota of Argentina

Mi-BEAR

  Citation suggested:

   Sanabria MCV, Coviella CE, Tolosa G (2025) Interactive Map of the Soil Biota of Argentina (Mi-BEAR).

   Available at: https://mi-bear-inedes.github.io/

Description

Mi -BEAR brings the information on Clitellata (earthworms), Acari (mites), and Collembola (springtails) reported in scientific publications and communications.A complete description of the methodology and the full dataset can be found in Sanabria et al.,2023.

The extension of Argentine Republic is 3,669,711 km², including terrestrial areas and maritime zones such as the South Orkney Islands and the South Atlantic islands. The country has a wide surface coverage; therefore, it also has important climatic diversity, ranging from the tropical climates of the Chaco, Tucumán-Oranense and Misiones ecoregions, to the cold and dry climate of Patagonia, which thus provides different environments for the fauna.
